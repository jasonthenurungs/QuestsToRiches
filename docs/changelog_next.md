### Changes
- Holdings tab, detail view: amounts of 10 million gold or more are shortened so they fit their columns.
  Daily change shows "+12.3M", and gold and the total show "12,345k".
- Navigation: turning War Mode on or off during navigation re-plans the remaining route right away.
  Quests whose reward stops being gold in War Mode are dropped, and quests that become gold again are added.
- Riches tab: the Liadrin weekly quest no longer shows characters as "Done" when they have not done it
  (for example in a week with no Liadrin weekly). Only completions seen by the addon are counted now.
